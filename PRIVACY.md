# Privacy Policy for Gematria Calculator

**Last Updated:** December 10, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by the Gematria Calculator website.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personally identifiable information to use the basic features of our calculator.

### Information Collected Automatically

*   **Local Storage:** To enhance your user experience, we use your browser's `localStorage` to save your settings, such as your selected Gematria systems and theme preference (light/dark mode). This data is stored only on your computer and is not transmitted to our servers.

*   **Third-Party Services:** We use third-party services to help us operate and improve our website. These services may collect information sent by your browser as part of a web page request, such as cookies or your IP address.

## Third-Party Services

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how our users engage with the website. Google Analytics collects information anonymously, such as the pages you visit, the time you spend on the site, and the type of browser you are using. This helps us improve the website and offer a better user experience.

You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps by visiting www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/.

### Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact form on our website or by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
