Unlocking Classical Foundations: Greek Isopsephy

For centuries, the numerical study of sacred literature relied on the Greek Milesian/Ionic numeral system—known as Isopsephy (from the Greek iso meaning "equal" and psephos meaning "pebble"). In v1.2.2, we bring full mathematical precision for classical Greek words and biblical manuscripts into the platform:

  • The 27-Character System: Accurately calculates units (1–9), tens (10–90), and hundreds (100–900).
  • Archaic Numeral Characters: Complete support for historical obsolete letters: Digamma/Stigma (Ϝ, Ϛ, ϛ = 6), Koppa (Ϙ, Ϟ, ϟ = 90), and Sampi (Ͳ, ͳ, Ϡ, ϡ = 900).
  • Sigma Adaptability: Full native support for medial sigma (σ), final terminal sigma (ς), and epigraphic lunate sigma (Ϲ, ϲ) at 200.
  • Polytonic Diacritic Normalization: Our input engine decomposes and strips combining Greek breathings, accents, and subscript marks. Paste authentic polytonic Greek directly from the Septuagint, New Testament, or historical papyri with 100% calculation accuracy.
